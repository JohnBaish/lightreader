Lightreader · compare

Three ways to compare

Each option shows the compare section as it would sit below the sky ribbon. All the figures are real, for Reading (RG1) on Tuesday October 27th 2026, worked out to the second and then rounded.

The examples differ on purpose, so you can see each kind of sentence: a date ahead (Christmas Day), another place (Aberdeen) and a date behind with the clock change (last July 14th).

Option A

One box for everything

A single box that takes either a date or a postcode and works out which you meant. Quick choices sit underneath, and the page always says back what it understood.

Computer · Christmas Day
Phone · Christmas Day
Option B

Date or place, side by side

Two clear modes with a switch between them. The computer frame shows the date mode; the phone frame shows the place mode, comparing with Aberdeen.

Computer · Another date
Phone · Another place
Option C

Paired ribbons

The single box from A, with two small sky ribbons drawn on the same midnight-to-midnight scale above the sentences. You see the difference before you read it.

Computer · last July 14th
Phone · last July 14th

Notes

These are light mode only. Dark versions of the chosen option can follow, using the colours you approved.

Differences are worked out from the exact times, so now and then a sum will look a minute out, as agreed.

Typing "July 14th" alone would mean the next one. The C example assumes you typed "last July 14th".

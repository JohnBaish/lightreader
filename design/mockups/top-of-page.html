Lightreader: three ways to open the page

Each option shows the top of the page: today, the next 24 hours, and the first two groups of sentences. All the figures are real, for Reading (RG1) on Tuesday October 27th 2026 at 09:02.

Each one is drawn at true computer width (1100px) and phone width (390px), then scaled to fit your screen. Dark-mode versions of A and B follow B.

Option A

The running order

A plain list of what happens next, one row per change of light, like a programme running order. Easy to scan, and the most like Hindcaster.

The sky ribbon

The next 24 hours as a strip of colour, drawn to scale, so you can see how long each stretch of light lasts. On a phone the strip turns upright and the long night is folded up.

The five band colours, from sunshine yellow through to a deep night blue, are new. They would join the house palette, so they need your approval.

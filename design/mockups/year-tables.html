Three ways to show the year

Each option covers six months back and six ahead, from April 27th 2026 to April 27th 2027. They sit at the bottom of the page, below Compare.

All the figures are real, for Reading (RG1), and come from Astronomy Engine, the library now recommended. Today, Tuesday October 27th, is highlighted.

Option A

Week by week

One row a week, on the same weekday as today, so the whole year is about 52 rows. Month chips jump through it. On a phone, each week becomes a small card.

Phone · October to December
Option B

Day by day, a month at a time

Every day of one month, with arrows to step through the 13 months. This shows the most detail, including half-light and when it gets very dark.

Computer · October 2026
Phone · October 2026
Option C

The year ribbon

Every day drawn as a thin upright strip of the band colours, from midnight at the bottom to midnight at the top. The whole year becomes one picture, and you tap a day to see its figures. The two clock changes show as steps.
